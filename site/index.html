Give your medicine
its own moment.

Wait 60 minutes before breakfast or coffee. This plan keeps milk/whey four hours after thyroxine.

Why the gap?

Calcium and iron can bind to levothyroxine in the gut and reduce absorption. Separate calcium/iron supplements by at least four hours. Guidance for calcium-rich foods varies; this routine follows the conservative four-hour approach. Confirm your tablet’s instructions with your pharmacist and keep habits consistent.

This explores the calorie budget; it does not measure your metabolism or prescribe a medical diet.

01

Macros build the foundation.

Protein supports repair. Carbs fuel training. Fats support essential functions. Your estimated BMR is about 1,740 kcal; daily needs are higher.

02

Micros fill in the details.

Vitamins and minerals support bones, blood, and energy metabolism. Pay attention to B12, iron, calcium, and vitamin D in a vegetarian diet.

03

Fibre is part of the picture.

It is a type of carbohydrate that supports digestion and gut health. Dal, oats, fruit, vegetables, and whole grains all count.
