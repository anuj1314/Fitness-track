# Daily routine: morning gym and office

## Current preferences

- Leave home for office at **10:00 am**; all times below are Bengaluru local time
  (IST).
- Gym is approximately **100 metres from home**; allow one hour for training.
- Running is paused and is not included in this routine.
- Follow a vegetarian diet without curd or yogurt; buttermilk is acceptable.
- Prescribed thyroxine dose: **50 micrograms (µg/mcg)** each morning for
  hypothyroidism. Follow the prescriber's and pharmacist's instructions.

## Daily timeline

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| **6:00 am** | Wake up; take the prescribed **50 µg thyroxine with plain water**, on an empty stomach. |
| **7:00 am** | Light breakfast: 2–3 idlis with sambar, or a small serving of vegetable poha/upma. |
| **7:45–8:45 am** | Gym session. |
| **8:45–9:15 am** | Walk home, cool down, and shower. |
| **9:15–9:45 am** | Get ready and pack lunch and office snacks. |
| **10:00 am** | Second meal: milk/whey, fruit, and roasted chana; leave for office. Prepare it ahead or carry it if needed to leave on time. |
| **1:00 pm** | Lunch: chapatis/rice, dal or beans, tofu, vegetables, and optional buttermilk. |
| **4:00 pm** | Afternoon snack: whey or another protein source with bread/fruit, adjusted to the day's protein target. |
| **7:30–8:30 pm** | Dinner: soy chunks, tofu, or paneer with chapatis/rice, vegetables, and optional buttermilk. |
| **9:30–10:00 pm** | Bedtime, allowing approximately 8–8½ hours in bed before the 6 am wake-up. |

## Medication and training timing

- The schedule allows **60 minutes between thyroxine and breakfast**. Take the
  tablet with water; wait until after this interval for coffee as well.
- The 10 am milk/whey snack is **four hours after the tablet**. Keep calcium and
  iron supplements at least four hours apart from thyroxine. Confirm food
  spacing and any other medication interactions with your pharmacist for your
  specific product.
- Thyroxine itself does not require a waiting period before exercise. The
  breakfast-to-gym interval is for digestive comfort, not a medication rule.
- Keep breakfast light. If 45 minutes is too short for comfortable training,
  reduce the portion or allow more digestion time within the morning schedule.
- You do not need a protein shake immediately after the gym. The later snack
  fits this routine; total daily protein and consistency matter more.
- Keep medication timing and eating habits consistent. Follow clinician-directed
  thyroid monitoring; do not adjust the medication dose based on weight or gym
  performance.

## Nutrition and recovery

- With running paused, use roughly **2,900 kcal/day as an initial trial**, not a
  measured requirement. Aim for **150–170 g protein/day**. Exact calorie needs
  depend on daily movement, workout intensity, thyroid control, and weight trend.
- Moving milk/whey from breakfast to the second meal changes the earlier diet
  template. Count your actual portions so protein and calories still meet your
  targets; do not add every old breakfast item on top of this routine.
- Compare weekly average weights over **2–3 weeks**. For gradual muscle gain,
  aim for about **0.08–0.20 kg/week** at 78 kg. Adjust calories by **100–150/day**
  if the trend is consistently below or above the intended rate.
- Gym attendance does not require hard lifting every day. Include easier
  sessions or rest days according to your training plan and recovery.
- Aim for **7–9 hours of actual sleep**. Move bedtime earlier if needed to allow
  time to fall asleep.
